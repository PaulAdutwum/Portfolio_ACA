About Me

From Ghana to Maine: A Journey of Excellence

My journey began in Ghana, where I developed a passion for mathematics and technology. This passion led me to Bates College in Maine, where I'm pursuing degrees in Physics and Mathematics while making significant contributions to both academic research and community development.

As a researcher in computational number theory, I've published work on Ulam words and their applications, contributing to the mathematical community's understanding of these fascinating sequences.

My engineering work spans supply chain optimization, computer vision, and AI security, always with the goal of building practical solutions that make a real difference in people's lives.

Ubuntu Philosophy

"I am because we are" - This African philosophy guides my approach to community building and leadership, emphasizing our interconnectedness and shared humanity.
